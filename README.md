# ⚽ Penalty Shootout – Ultimate Football Challenge

A fun and interactive browser-based football penalty shootout game built using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**. Test your shooting skills, outsmart the goalkeeper, and score as many goals as possible in five penalty shots!

## 🎮 About the Project

Penalty Shootout is a lightweight football game where players choose the direction of their shots and try to score against a goalkeeper that randomly selects a direction to make a save.

The game features a football-themed interface, an interactive scoreboard, shooting animations, and a restart option for unlimited replayability.

## ✨ Features

* ⚽ **Interactive Gameplay:** Shoot the ball towards the left, center, or right.
* 🧤 **Goalkeeper AI:** The goalkeeper randomly chooses a direction to save the shot.
* 🏆 **Live Scoreboard:** Track goals, saves, and remaining shots.
* 🎯 **Five-Shot Challenge:** Score as many goals as possible in five attempts.
* 🎨 **Football Stadium Design:** Enjoy a green football pitch and a stadium-inspired interface.
* 🔄 **Restart Option:** Start a new game whenever you want.
* 📱 **Responsive Design:** Play on desktop, tablet, or mobile screens.
* ⚡ **Lightweight:** Built with vanilla JavaScript without external libraries.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open the game in your browser.
2. Choose a shooting direction: **Left, Center, or Right**.
3. Watch the goalkeeper attempt to save your shot.
4. Score goals whenever the goalkeeper dives in the wrong direction.
5. Complete all five shots and check your final score.
6. Click **Restart Game** to play again.

### 🏅 Scoring

* **Goal:** Your shot goes past the goalkeeper.
* **Save:** The goalkeeper correctly predicts your shooting direction.
* **Victory:** Score four or five goals to earn the game's top achievement message.
